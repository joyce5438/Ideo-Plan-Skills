# Regression cases

1. A food series had a regional → urban structure. Do not turn it into a default outline for different topics.
2. Broad preliminary topic research returned shallow viral claims. Route source selection by question and check original-language sources; mark uncertain claims.
3. User asked to discuss first. Do not draft a complete article or begin dependent deep research while awaiting a required direction.
4. Research uncovers a useful unexpected angle. Do not silently discard it or imply the user had requested it; present evidence and distinguish inference.
5. User gives a fully specified direct request. Do not demand a needless approval checkpoint.
6. Research changes an agreed central question. Do not quietly change scope; explain the evidence and await a decision on dependent work.
